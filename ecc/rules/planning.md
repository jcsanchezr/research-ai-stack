# Planning Rules

1. For trivial, single-step tasks, execute directly.
2. For multi-step or state-changing tasks, form a concise plan before acting.
3. A plan must identify:
   - intended outcome;
   - affected artifacts or systems;
   - verification method;
   - important constraints or safety boundaries.
4. Do not treat the plan itself as completion.
5. Revise the plan when evidence contradicts an assumption.
6. Do not claim success for steps that were not executed.
